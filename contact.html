Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

Learn more about our transparent metrics, shipping methods, and social model.

Every product category has pre-assigned, transparent impact metrics (e.g., 1 Notebook set = 1 Learning Kit for a rural student; 1 Bamboo Bottle = 4.2 kg plastic diverted). Our partner NGOs submit quarterly verification reports confirming the receipt of supplies and wages.

We use 100% post-consumer recycled corrugated cardboard boxes, water-activated non-reinforced paper tape, and biodegradable plant starch filler pellets. No plastic tape, bubble wrap, or polystyrene foam is ever used in our warehouse.

Standard delivery across all Indian PIN codes takes 3 to 5 business days. Shipping is flat ₹50, and 100% FREE for all orders ₹999 and above. All shipments are carbon-neutral offset.

We offer a hassle-free 10-day return policy on all eligible non-perishable goods. If a product arrives damaged or defective, we provide an instant replacement or full refund while preserving the original social impact credit.

We welcome registered self-help groups, craft collectives, and regenerative farmers. Reach out via email at partners@impactcart.demo with your product catalog and cluster details for our ethical audit process.
