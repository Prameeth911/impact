Behind the Numbers

How Different Product Categories Create Impact

We convert everyday commerce into direct community funding through four clear mechanisms.

📚 Education Products

Demo Rule: +10 to +18 Points
Each notebook, solar study lamp, or plantable kit funds stationery and solar reading lights for students in rural classrooms.

🌱 Eco-Friendly Products

Demo Rule: +8 to +16 Points
Calculated by plastic weight avoided and organic materials substituted. Replaces single-use plastics with natural bamboo & jute.

🎨 Handmade Crafts

Demo Rule: +7 to +14 Points
Guarantees ethical hourly compensation to pottery guilds, hand-block printers, and woodcarvers without middlemen.

🤝 Community Initiatives

Demo Rule: +9 to +20 Points
Provides healthcare fund subsidies and clean drinking water access for smallholder organic farmers and weaving collectives.
